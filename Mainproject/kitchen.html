A fly works a station only when its own readout neuron is firing. Arriving at the rice boiler drives that fly's real LB3a water cells; about 25 ms of simulated time later DNg67 fires, and that firing is what commits a tick of work. A quiet readout stalls the step even though the body is standing there.

These are the controls, and they are part of the product rather than a test script. Lesion silences every station's sensory cells: the flies still fly to their stations and then stand there, because nothing reaches a motor neuron. Scramble rewires the graph degree-preservingly — each neuron keeps its out-degree and its weights, but they land on random partners — which is what shows the measured structure is doing the work and not just the cell count.
